# Ruling: achievement 100010 star total (user, 2026-11-18)

Amends ACHIEVEMENT_EVENT_FACTS_V1, row 100010.

The 100010 fact is the sum of the best star count over the playable nodes of the Normal, Super and Challenge
campaign chains (155 + 155 + 144 nodes, content ceiling 1,362), not the Normal-only sum the client's TotalStars
shows (ceiling 465, which leaves tiers 600 and 1200 unreachable). The achievement text "Clear all campaigns and
earn %d stars" is the evidence; the user approved it on 2026-11-18. Implemented by I9_11E_ACHIEVEMENTS_V2 C9.
